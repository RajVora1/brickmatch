          How it works

          Two small neural networks do the seeing. Everything after that is plain counting and search.

          1. 1

            Find every piece

            A YOLO detector, fine-tuned on hand-labeled pile photos, draws a box around each piece. It only learns one thing: "this is a piece".

          2. 2

            Name each one

            Each box is cropped and a ResNet-18 classifier decides which of 8 piece types it is: bricks 1x1 to 2x4, a 1x2 plate and two slopes.

          3. 3

            Read the color

            The middle of each crop is matched to the closest of 8 brick colors. No training needed for that part.

          4. 4

            Match builds

            Your counts are compared with the parts list of every build, ranked by how many pieces you already have, with the missing ones listed.

          96%of pieces in real photos named right (115 of 120 test crops)
          99.9%of pieces found on a held-out pile (recall; pieces weren't touching)
          24hand-designed builds, each with a checked layout, 3D view and booklet

          Tips for a good photo

          • Plain, even background (a table, a sheet of paper)
          • Pieces spread out, not touching or stacked
          • Shoot from straight above, in good light
          • Fill the frame with the pieces
